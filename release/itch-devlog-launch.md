# Minefield: Beachhead is live

Minefield: Beachhead is a tactical browser minesweeper about pressure, signal reads, and one wrong step.

You can play directly in the browser on desktop or mobile. No download required.

## What is in this release

- Classic mode: a clean one-life minefield with simple rules and hard consequences.
- Campaign mode: five escalating sectors with Focus pressure, SCAN reads, supplies, route choices, and Field Post upgrades.
- Mobile web support: portrait layout, landscape fallback, touch controls, long-press markers, and PWA-style Add to Home Screen support where your browser allows it.
- Local campaign progress: supplies and upgrades are saved in your browser storage.
- Browser SFX: sound unlocks after the first player gesture and can be toggled with the A ON/OFF HUD control.

## Controls

- Click or tap: reveal a tile.
- Right click: place a marker.
- Long press on mobile: place a marker.
- Space: activate SCAN in Campaign.
- Operator button: restart or return after failure.

## A note on mobile

This is a mobile web game, not a native Android APK or iOS app. Open the itch.io page in your phone browser, use fullscreen when available, and use Add to Home Screen if you want a quick app-like shortcut.
