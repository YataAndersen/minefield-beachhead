Recruit Manual

HOW TO PLAY

Read the field, mark the danger, and only open tiles when the numbers give you permission.

Classic

  • Open tiles to reveal numbers. Each number tells how many mines touch that tile.
  • Use the numbers to deduce safe tiles. Opening a mine ends the run immediately.
  • Mark suspected mines with flags so you do not step there later.
  • Clear every safe tile to win the field.

Campaign

  • Fight through five sectors. Each sector has more mines and stronger pressure.
  • Focus is your campaign health. Mistakes and SCAN spend focus; reaching zero ends the operation.
  • SCAN reveals safe signals when the field gets too dangerous to read cleanly.
  • Earn supplies, choose routes between sectors, and buy Field Post upgrades to survive later sectors.
Desktop: click to open a tile, right-click to flag. Mobile: tap to open, long press to flag.
